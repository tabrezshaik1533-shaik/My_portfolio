// 01 — SUMMARY

Who Am I?

Hi, I'm Shaik Tabrez, a B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) at Audisankara Institute of Technology, Gudur, with an expected graduation in 2027 and a current CGPA of 7.9.

I chose AIML because I’m genuinely interested in how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be applied to solve real-world problems. The rapid growth of AI technologies and their potential applications motivated me to explore this field and build my skills around it.

Python is my primary programming language, and I have hands-on experience with HTML, CSS, Django, Flask, and Machine Learning. I also have a basic understanding of Java and have been exploring Generative AI and Prompt Engineering.

I have gained practical exposure through two internships. I completed a 2-month Software Programmer – Python internship at CSC India, where I strengthened my Python programming and software development skills. I also completed a 2-month Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning internship at SmartBridge, where I gained practical exposure to AI/ML concepts and applications.

As part of my academic and personal learning, I have built projects such as a Student Management System using Python and a Spotify UI/UX application. These projects have helped me improve my programming, problem-solving, and design skills while giving me experience turning ideas into working projects.

I enjoy learning new technologies and continuously improving my technical skills. My current career interests are Python Development, Backend Development, AI/ML, and Software Development, and I’m focused on building practical projects and gaining the industry experience needed to grow as a professional developer.
